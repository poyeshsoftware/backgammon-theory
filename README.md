# Backgammon Theory: Stochastic State Trees, Markov Absorbing Chains, and Doubling Cube Economics

Open-source research monographs on the mathematical foundations, computational complexity, and systems engineering of modern backgammon engines.

Published by **Poyesh Software Research Group** · 2026.

---

## Monographs in this Repository

### 1. [Stochastic State Trees, Expectiminimax Horizons, and Cryptographic Commit-Reveal Dice Protocols in Modern Backgammon Engines](https://poyeshsoftware.github.io/backgammon-theory/)
* **Focus:** Combinatorial complexity, chance nodes ($|\Omega| = 21$), branching factors, star-pruning (*1/*2), TD-Gammon neural evaluation vectors, and zero-trust commit-reveal dice protocols.
* **File:** [`index.html`](index.html) | [`stochastic-state-trees-commit-reveal-dice.md`](stochastic-state-trees-commit-reveal-dice.md)

### 2. [Absorbing Markov Chains, Fundamental Matrices, and Exact Analytical Bearoff Solvers](https://poyeshsoftware.github.io/backgammon-theory/absorbing-markov-chains-bearoff-solvers.html)
* **Focus:** Disengaged race states, 54,264-state absorbing Markov chain, fundamental matrix $N = (I - Q)^{-1}$, absorption time PMF/CDF, mutual race inner products, and pip wastage phenomena.
* **File:** [`absorbing-markov-chains-bearoff-solvers.html`](absorbing-markov-chains-bearoff-solvers.html) | [`absorbing-markov-chains-bearoff-solvers.md`](absorbing-markov-chains-bearoff-solvers.md)

### 3. [Continuous Financial Options Pricing, Janowski Match Equity Formulas, and Doubling Cube Economics](https://poyeshsoftware.github.io/backgammon-theory/doubling-cube-options-pricing-janowski.html)
* **Focus:** Doubling cube as an American call option, the 25% take point derivation, live vs dead cube volatility drag, Janowski match equity formulas, Crawford rule, and post-Crawford free drops.
* **File:** [`doubling-cube-options-pricing-janowski.html`](doubling-cube-options-pricing-janowski.html) | [`doubling-cube-options-pricing-janowski.md`](doubling-cube-options-pricing-janowski.md)
